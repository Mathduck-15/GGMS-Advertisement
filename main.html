What GGMS does

Clear tools built around the way barangays and municipal offices already work together.

  • Role-based loginBarangay and municipal users see only what their role needs.
  • Electronic submissionApplications and documents go in digitally, with no extra trips.
  • Automatic routingEach request reaches the proper municipal office.
  • Approve or rejectReviewers record a clear decision on every application.
  • Real-time statusCheck progress any time without calling or visiting.
  • Transaction recordsEvery step is logged for transparent, accountable service.
